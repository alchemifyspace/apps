Glamio: bookings for salons, without the phone calls

Android and iPhone · Web admin · English, Spanish, French and Arabic · Light and dark

Three apps in one product

For clients

  • Browse services without an account
  • Book one or more services together
  • Choose a stylist, or "anyone available"
  • See only times that are really free
  • Move or cancel, with the salon's rules
  • Booking history and reminders

For stylists

  • Their own day, updated live
  • Client name, phone and notes
  • Mark visits completed or no-show
  • Add walk-in clients
  • Push notice for new and moved bookings

For the owner (web admin)

  • Dashboard: bookings, revenue, no-show rate
  • Day and week calendar by stylist
  • Walk-in and phone bookings
  • Services, prices, staff, hours, time off
  • Closures and booking rules
  • Stylist portfolios and app access

Built to be trusted

No double bookings

The database itself refuses two bookings for the same stylist at the same time, even if two clients tap "Book" in the same second.

Time zones done right

Free times are worked out in the salon's own time zone, including daylight-saving days, and tested.

Secure by default

Row-level security on every table: a client sees only their own bookings, staff only their salon. Tested with 196 database tests.

Store-ready

In-app account deletion and a public delete-account page, as Google Play and the App Store require.

Easy to rebrand

Name, colours, logo, words and features live in one place. No "salon" words are hard-coded in the booking engine.

Clean code

Riverpod without code generation, a repository layer, no analyzer warnings, and automated tests for the app, the admin and the database.

Technology

PartBuilt with
Phone appFlutter (Android and iPhone), Riverpod, go_router, slang translations, Material 3
Web adminNext.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, next-intl
BackendSupabase: Postgres with row-level security, Auth (email code, Google), Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions, pg_cron
Push notificationsFirebase Cloud Messaging, sent from a Supabase Edge Function
HostingSupabase (free plan works to start), web admin on Vercel or Netlify

What you need

  • A Supabase account (free plan is enough to start)
  • Flutter and Node.js on your computer (the docs walk you through it)
  • A Vercel or Netlify account for the web admin (free)
  • Google Play and Apple developer accounts to publish to the stores

Good to know

  • Push notifications are tested on Android; on iPhone they need your Apple Developer account
  • Online payments are not included in this version
  • One business per installation
  • Installation and custom features are available as a paid service
