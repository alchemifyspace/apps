1.0.0 Coming soon

First release.

  • Client app: browse services, book one or more services, choose a stylist or "anyone", see free times, confirm, move and cancel, booking history, profile and account deletion.
  • Stylist app: live schedule, booking details, completed and no-show, walk-in bookings.
  • Web admin: dashboard, day and week calendar, walk-in and phone bookings, services, stylists, working hours, time off, closures, clients, settings, stylist portfolios, app access for staff.
  • Notifications: push for new, moved and cancelled bookings, and reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before a visit.
  • Sign-in: email code on every phone, plus Google sign-in on Android (optional).
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French and Arabic (right to left). Light and dark themes.
  • Quality: double bookings blocked by the database, free times worked out in the salon's time zone, row-level security on every table, automated tests for the app, the admin and the database.
